About CutLoom Studio

CutLoom is a high-performance, web-based video editing suite built with HTML5, WebGL, and JavaScript.

Designed for fast in-browser video processing, CutLoom allows users to trim clips, apply filters, and render video content directly on their local device without requiring software downloads or backend server uploads.

Privacy Policy

At CutLoom, we prioritize your privacy. All video processing and rendering occur locally in your web browser. We do not store or upload your personal media files to external servers.
